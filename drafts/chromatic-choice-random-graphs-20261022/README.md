# The Choice Number of Random Graphs

**Lanchao Wang and Jiasheng Zeng**

A preprint on **list colouring of random graphs**, proving the conjecture of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov. For every edge-probability sequence satisfying `np → ∞`, with high probability,

$$
\operatorname{ch}(G(n,p))=(1+o(1))\,\chi(G(n,p)),
\qquad np\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

The proof treats growing and bounded independent sets separately. In the first case, a uniform independent-set estimate sharpens the Krivelevich–Vu method. In the second, a deterministic theorem derives a list colouring from a proper colouring with bounded colour classes and a uniform independent-set condition. Its proof uses random vertex sampling and Kahn's matching theorem. First-moment and Janson estimates complete the random-graph application.
