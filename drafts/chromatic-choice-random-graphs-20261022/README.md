# Asymptotic equality of chromatic and choice numbers in random graphs

**Lanchao Wang and Jiasheng Zeng**

A research manuscript on **list colouring of random graphs** and the conjecture of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov. We prove that the choice number and chromatic number of the binomial random graph are asymptotically equal for every edge-probability sequence satisfying `np → ∞`.

$$
\operatorname{ch}(G(n,p))=(1+o_{\mathbb P}(1))\,\chi(G(n,p)),
\qquad np\longrightarrow\infty.
$$

The proof splits into two regimes. When the typical colour-class size grows, we establish a uniform independent-set estimate and apply the list-colouring method of Krivelevich and Vu. When colour classes have bounded size, we develop a deterministic clique-partition-to-list-colouring result using random sampling of clique packings, a fractional matching and Kahn's matching theorem. First-moment and Janson estimates complete the random-graph application.
