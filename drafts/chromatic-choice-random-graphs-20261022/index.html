Asymptotic equality of chromatic and choice numbers in random graphs

Lanchao Wang and Jiasheng Zeng · Research manuscript · October 2026

Read the PDF manuscript · LaTeX source · Overview and keywords

Main result

We prove the conjecture of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov that, for the binomial random graph G(n,p), the choice number and chromatic number are asymptotically equal whenever np → ∞.

ch(G(n,p)) = (1 + o𝔓(1)) χ(G(n,p)).

Proof overview

The argument separates regimes according to the typical colour-class size. For growing classes, a uniform independent-set estimate permits an application of the Krivelevich–Vu list-colouring procedure. For bounded classes, a deterministic proposition converts clique partitions in the complementary graph into list colourings. Its proof samples clique packings to construct a fractional matching with small weighted codegrees, then applies Kahn's matching theorem. First-moment and Janson estimates finish the random-graph application.
