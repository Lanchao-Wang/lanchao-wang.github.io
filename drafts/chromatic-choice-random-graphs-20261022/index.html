The Choice Number of Random Graphs

Lanchao Wang and Jiasheng Zeng · arXiv:2610.35003 · v1 submitted October 26, 2026

arXiv abstract · arXiv PDF · arXiv v1 source · Version comparison and keywords

Status: arXiv preprint, 12 pages in v1; not yet peer reviewed.

Main result

We prove the conjecture of Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov that, for the binomial random graph G(n,p), the choice number and chromatic number are asymptotically equal whenever np → ∞.

ch(G(n,p)) = (1 + o(1)) χ(G(n,p)), with high probability.

Proof overview

The proof treats growing and bounded independent sets separately. In the first case, a uniform independent-set estimate sharpens the Krivelevich–Vu list-colouring method. In the second, Theorem 1.3 derives a list colouring from a proper colouring with bounded colour classes under a uniform independent-set condition. Random vertex sampling and Kahn's matching theorem colour almost all vertices. First-moment and Janson estimates complete the random-graph application.
